# Nanyang Wharf

Nanyang Wharf (南洋码头) is the dock company of [Liankou](liankou.md). It berths the export hulls, issues the dock token, and charges demurrage on anything the [Seal Office](seal-office.md) has not yet stamped. The wharf is a joint stock of [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) and an overseas Chinese shipping line. The line supplies the hulls. Huanan supplies the seal.

A token from the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) opens a berth. A token that fails mid-shift leaves the hull on the clock. The clock is Nanyang's. Haulers call the fee 滞期, demurrage, whether or not they are the ones who missed the stamp.

The wharf does not employ the lower city. It permits it, by the shift. Residence is a [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) word, 户口. Nanyang does not print 户口.
