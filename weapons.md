# Armaments in Liankou

**Armaments in Liankou** are the named firearms and directed-energy weapons in use inside the special zone. Inventory follows the stack. Below the service band the arms are conventional 1980s firearms and mill tools. Above the band they are higher-grade firearms and a small number of directed-energy weapons that do not circulate in the lower stack.

## Lower stack

- **[Type 77 pistol](type-77.md)** (77式手枪). Compact domestic sidearm. Issued to [Zone Protection](zone-protection.md) details on the lower ribs and to cadre escorts. The most common pistol below the band. [Liang Zhi](protagonist.md) left the freight apron with one.
- **Type 64 pistol** (64式手枪). Older domestic pistol still in circulation. Carried by landing bosses and by men who cannot get a 77.
- **12-gauge pump shotgun**. Domestic and imported pumps. The common long arm on a landing. Break-action single- and double-barrel guns are also used.
- **Type 81 rifle** (81式步枪). Semi-automatic rifle. Logged to Protection details for seizures. Copies and taken examples appear below the band.
- **Pneumatic driver**. Mill and harbor tool. Not classified as a weapon. Used as one at corridor range.

## Service band

- Type 77 and Type 81 remain the Protection issue for work on the band.
- Licensed pumps and rifles move under a firm stamp. [White Crane Association](white-crane.md) lieutenants handle most of that traffic.

## Upper city

- **[QSZ-85 pistol](qsz-85.md)** (higher-grade domestic and joint-venture sidearm). Issued to crown Protection details and to firm security. Suppressed examples are held by [Liankou Development](huanan-development.md) site offices.
- **Imported rifle, 5.56**. Logged against private houses for gallery security. Does not leave the sealed floors on paper.
- **[Regulation beam](regulation-beam.md)** (调节束). Short-range directed-energy weapon. Held by crown Protection and by two firm security offices. Power-hungry. Not issued below the service band. None logged as lost.
- **Gallery crew gun**. Crew-served conventional piece kept in sealed upper galleries. Never issued to a landing.

The struggle session and firing squad that sentenced Liang were carried out with Type 77 pistols and Type 81 rifles. No Regulation beam was present.
