# Liankou Pour

Liankou Pour (联口浇筑) is the slab company. It frames dormitory blocks between contract cycles and books the pour in the [Forward Book](forward-book.md) before the concrete is up. A landing becomes an address because mail and shake-downs both need a name. Pour does not name it. The landing does.

The company is paid by [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) against a clearance, not against a finished floor. Stairs cut where the drawings showed ducts are outside the contract. Weather in the 下栈 is condensate off coils Pour did not install and does not service.

Pour crews are 临时工, like the mills. Their permits expire when the slab is booked, which is often before it can be stood on.
