# Government of Liankou

Liankou has two authorities on one map, and three houses that do the daily work of the split.

The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) is the political administration. It posts targets, tours the atria, and uses the language of the opening: development of the productive forces, regulation through the market, a temporary exception. It describes the lower city as transitional housing.

[Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) holds the concession instrument, the inference halls, the export seals, and private law in Shangcheng. It is older than the zone. It does not call itself a government. It prices the lower city: shifts, bunks, dock tokens, water pressure, variance reports.

Under them, the [Seal Office](seal-office.md) rents the gate, the [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md) houses the rotation and the trap, and the [White Crane Association](white-crane.md) sells the protection both would rather buy. House security stands at the service band. Crane desks stand beside it. Neither patrols the ribs.

A stamp is still required to make a status real. Above the service band, stamps are a market. A runner sent down with a real stamp and a rewritten token is the ordinary instrument of a cancellation.
