# Huaqiao Remittance

Huaqiao Remittance (华侨汇兑) is the overseas Chinese money house of [Liankou](liankou.md). It converts foreign bills and family drafts into zone scrip, and zone scrip into the convertible balance a cadre is not supposed to take home. The [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md) keeps an account here. So does [White Crane](white-crane.md).

The house calls the instrument 汇款, a remittance. In Shangcheng it is a market. In the lower city it is a rumor: a cousin's draft that arrived, a fee that ate it, a second book. Huaqiao does not send money below the service band. It sells the right to be told that it did.

Huanan does not own the house. It licenses the window. A second window would be a second system, which the [Seal Office](seal-office.md) does not allow at the gate and does not bother to forbid in a ledger.
