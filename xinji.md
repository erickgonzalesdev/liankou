# Xinji Electronics

Xinji Electronics (新纪电子) is a private assembly house in Shangcheng. It builds inference boards, terminal racks, and the rebuilt cathode tubes that later show up in the 下栈. The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) is a customer. It is not the owner.

Xinji's capital is a Hong Kong line and a local partner who was a cadre and did not go home. The company leases hall space from [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) and refuses the offered merger twice a year. Huanan answers by slowing seals on Xinji crates. Xinji answers by selling the same boards through buyers who already have an hour.

The live weights stay in Huanan's halls. Xinji sells the machines and the spares, and does not set the weights.
