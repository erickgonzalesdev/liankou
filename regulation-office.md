# Regulation Office

The Regulation Office (调节办公室) is the coordination organ of [Liankou](liankou.md). It was commissioned in 1978 as a cybernetic logistics office in the viable-system mold, then rebuilt around fifth-generation expert systems. Mills, gates, dormitory slabs, and customs desks report upward. The operations room in Shangcheng reduces that variety to allocations and sends them back down as tokens, rates, and quotas.

Doctrine calls this regulation by the market inside the plan: the market as the bird, the quota as the cage. Practice is narrower. The live weights optimize clearance and export yield. Labor, bunks, and water are inputs.

The zone committee describes the room as a socialist modernization of planning, pragmatic rather than orthodox. Huanan Development describes it as a leased instrument. Private courts in the upper city are outside its jurisdiction. The lower city is not. A failed dock token is a regulation event. Corporation records class it as output.

Operators call the crown installation the operations room. Lower-city usage is "the weights." Bootleg rule-weights circulate in the ribs. They rarely run.
