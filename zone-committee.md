# Zone Committee

The Liankou Zone Committee is the political administration of the special zone. It posts targets, tours the atria, and speaks the language of the opening: productive forces, regulation through the market, a temporary exception. It describes the lower city as transitional housing and the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) as a modernization of planning.

Cadres rotate in from the interior through the [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md). The committee issues their courtesy and their permits. [Liankou Development](huanan-development.md) keeps the sites and the seal. A canceled return is, on committee paper, a completed temporary posting.

## Campaigns

The committee publishes the zone's demonology. After the seizure of [Lixin Precision](lixin.md) it put [Liang Zhi](protagonist.md) on the sheets and the loudspeakers: enemy of the socialist road, thief of the people's boards, a face to report. The campaign is how a struck name stays a target after the permit has already been reduced to a number.
