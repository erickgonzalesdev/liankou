# Zone Committee

The Liankou Zone Committee is the political administration of the special zone. It posts targets, tours the atria, and speaks the language of the opening: productive forces, regulation through the market, a temporary exception. It describes the lower city as transitional housing and the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) as a modernization of planning.

Cadres rotate in from the interior through the [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md). The committee issues their courtesy. [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) issues the convertible balance. Whether the committee can countermand the corporation is not settled. The practical bargain is that the growth series will not be audited too closely if the operations room can still show a rising clearance.

The committee does not claim the private courts of Shangcheng. It claims the permits. A canceled return is, on committee paper, a completed temporary posting.
