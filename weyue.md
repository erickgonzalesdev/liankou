# Weyue Holdings

Weyue Holdings (伟裕实业) is a private holding company, registered outside the wall and trading inside it. It owns no charter. It owns slices: a cutting lien, a remittance desk, a block of bunk leases bought from small landlords when a permit wave failed.

Weyue's business is the spread. It buys a shift ticket low, after the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) has canceled the name, and sells the hour to [Desheng Mill](desheng.md) or to a shed that still needs the body. It does not employ the body. A ticket is not a 户口.

The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) calls it speculation. The service band calls it the other window. Huanan has tried to fold the desk into a side ledger. Weyue declines.
