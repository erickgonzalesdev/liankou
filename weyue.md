# Weyue Holdings

Weyue Holdings (伟裕实业) is a private holding company, registered outside the wall and trading inside it. It owns no charter. It owns slices: a cutting line, a remittance desk that is not [Huaqiao Remittance](huaqiao-remittance.md), a block of bunk leases bought from small landlords when a permit wave failed.

Weyue's business is the spread. It buys a shift ticket low, after the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) has canceled the name, and sells the hour high to a mill that still needs the body. It does not employ the body. Employment would be a 户口 problem. A ticket is not.

Huanan has tried to fold Weyue into the [Forward Book](forward-book.md) as a named side ledger. Weyue declines. The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) calls it speculation. The service band calls it the other window.
