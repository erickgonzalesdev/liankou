# Liankou Development Company

**Liankou Development Company** (联口工业发展总公司) is the state development corporation of the [Liankou Special Zone](liankou.md). It builds and leases sites inside the wall, holds the zone's only export seal, and receives firms seized by the [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md).

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | State-owned |
| Industry | Zone development, sites, export sealing |
| Founded | 1978 |
| Headquarters | Shangcheng, Liankou |
| Owner | Zone Committee (appoints the director) |

## History

The company was established with the opening to build the fence, berths, slabs, and sheds, then to rent them. Land inside the wall is its inventory. Foreign plants sign site contracts. Shed families pay rent until the lane is wanted for a larger contract.

## Operations

A foreign-exchange window and a single export stamp sit with the company. Slowing a stamp is how a refused site term is enforced. Growth figures used to extend the holiday are the company's series. A clearance may be entered when a contract is signed, before the crate exists. Seized assets, including [Lixin Precision](lixin.md), are vested here and retitled.

The company prints no 户口. Disputes go to the committee's economic section, staffed from the company. It has no separate private court.
