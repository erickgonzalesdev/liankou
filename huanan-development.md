# Liankou Development Company

The Liankou Development Company (联口工业发展总公司) is the zone's state development corporation. It was set up with the 1978 opening to build the fence, the berths, the slabs, and the factory sheds, then to rent them. It is not a private house and not a government. The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) appoints the director. The company keeps the books.

Land inside the wall is its inventory. A foreign plant does not buy a floor. It signs a site contract. A shed family does not own the lane. It pays rent until the site is wanted for a larger contract. The company prints no 户口. Temporary labor is the committee's permit and the company's shift.

The only export seal sits with the company because the zone was chartered that way: one window for foreign exchange, one stamp for a legal exit. Slowing a stamp is how a refused site term is enforced. There is no second gate. There is also no private court. Disputes go to the committee's economic section, which is staffed from the company.

Growth figures used to extend the holiday are the company's series. A clearance can be entered when the contract is signed, before the crate exists. The committee reads the series on tour.
