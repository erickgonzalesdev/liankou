# Minfa Steam

Minfa Steam (闽发轮船) is a private coastal line. The hulls are old. The manifests are clean enough for an hour at [Nanyang Wharf](nanyang-wharf.md) and no cleaner. Minfa pays berth fees, refuses Huanan's hull-sharing contract, and runs the night tide when Nanyang's token clock is closed to anyone but charter cargo.

The line hauls what the [Seal Office](seal-office.md) stamped late and what [Shunfa Trading](shunfa.md) could not get onto a Huanan hull. Crew are not zone labor. They are Minfa crew, paid in a scrip the [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md) will not take and a money changer will.

A Minfa captain who stays past the hour becomes a smuggler by the stamp clock, not by the cargo. The cargo was already legal. The berth was not.
