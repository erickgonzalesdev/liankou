# Minfa Lighters

**Minfa** (闽发驳船) is a small lighterage outfit in [Liankou](liankou.md). It does not hold a berth.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Private |
| Industry | Lighterage |
| Headquarters | Night side of the wall berths |

## Operations

Day berths belong to the [Harbor Bureau](harbor-bureau.md). Minfa takes late crates and is legal until the hour closes. Past the hour the same hull is smuggling by the clock, not necessarily by the cargo.
