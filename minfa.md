# Minfa Lighters

Minfa (闽发驳船) runs small lighters, not a steam line. Day berths belong to the [Harbor Bureau](harbor-bureau.md). Minfa takes the late crates [Shunfa Trading](shunfa.md) could not get on a foreign hull, and is legal until the hour closes.

Past the hour the same hull is smuggling. The cargo often is not. The clock is the bureau's.
