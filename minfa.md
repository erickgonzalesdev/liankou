# Minfa Steam

Minfa Steam (闽发轮船) is a private coastal line. The hulls are old. The manifests are clean enough for an hour at Huanan's berths and no cleaner. Minfa pays the berth fee, refuses the hull-sharing contract, and runs the night tide when the charter clock is closed to anyone but Huanan cargo.

The line hauls what [Shunfa Trading](shunfa.md) could not get onto a charter hull. Crew are Minfa crew, paid in a scrip the [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md) will not take.

A Minfa captain who stays past the hour becomes a smuggler by the stamp clock, not by the cargo. The cargo was already legal. The berth was not.
