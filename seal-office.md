# Seal Office

The Seal Office (印鉴处) is the only legal exit from [Liankou](liankou.md). Every shipment, permit hour, and hauler pass that leaves the wall takes its stamp. The office calls this universal clearance: one gate, one standard, no second system. The standard is the monopoly.

The office is an arm of [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md). It sits in Shangcheng, with a counter on the service band for those cleared for an hour. It owns the stamp, the ledger the stamp writes, and the line that carries a token from the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) to the gate. A landing boss cannot buy a seal. A [White Crane](white-crane.md) lieutenant can rent an hour, and resells it.

Clerks are the usual authors of a canceled return. A runner is sent down with a real stamp and a token already rewritten. The office records a completed errand. There is no second gate to appeal to. Restoration is a seal the office will not issue twice.

The zone committee tours the office as regulated export. The plaque in the atrium says one system, one clearance.
