# Seal Office

The Seal Office (印鉴处) is the customs and export-license arm of [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md). It holds the stamps that turn a shipment into a legal exit from [Liankou](liankou.md). A seal is harder to forge than to rent. Most of the office's crime is a rented stamp.

The office sits in Shangcheng, above the service band, with a counter on the band itself for haulers and brokers who are cleared for an hour. Below that counter the office does not stand. A landing boss cannot buy a seal. A [White Crane](white-crane.md) lieutenant can, and does, and resells the hour.

Clerks of the office are the usual authors of a canceled return. A runner is sent to the band or the stack with a real stamp and a token already rewritten. The office records a completed errand. The runner's name is a variance by the time he lands. Restoration, if it exists, is a seal the office will not issue twice.

The zone committee tours the office as proof of regulated export. Huanan accounts it as a lease on the gate.
