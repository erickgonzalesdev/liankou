# Economy of Liankou

Liankou runs a dual-track economy, 双轨制, that hardened into floors. Above the service band, 中带: contracts, convertible scrip, 合资 joint ventures, overseas Chinese capital, courts outside the plan. Below it: allocated beds, shift tickets, piece rates, quota. The same part can carry two prices depending on which side of a stair it stands on.

The licensed windows are listed under [market houses](corporations.md). [Nanyang Wharf](nanyang-wharf.md) berths the hulls. [Jinlu Mill](jinlu-mill.md) cuts the export. [Huaqiao Remittance](huaqiao-remittance.md) converts the bills. [Liankou Pour](pour-company.md) frames the slabs. All of them clear through the [Seal Office](seal-office.md), and all of them can be entered in the [Forward Book](forward-book.md) before the goods exist.

The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) is what keeps the two tracks from being called two economies. A price divergence is filed as managed variety. Credit, customs, piece-rate, bunk, and risk are scored continuously off the export feed. A 许可证 permits the shift. A 户口 is not included.
