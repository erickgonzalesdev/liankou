# Economy of Liankou

Liankou runs a dual-track economy that hardened into architecture. Above the service band: contracts, convertible scrip, joint ventures, outside and overseas Chinese capital, courts outside the plan. Below it: allocated beds, shift tickets, piece rates, quota. The same part can carry two prices depending on which side of a stair it stands on.

The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) is what keeps the two tracks from being called two economies. A price divergence is filed as managed variety. Credit, customs, piece-rate, bunk, and risk are scored continuously off the export feed.

Getting rich is treated in Shangcheng as evidence the method works. The method, one band down, is a token. Export figures are the city's political defense and its municipal account.
