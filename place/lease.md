# Lease

The Lease is a walled concession that outgrew its holiday. Chartered in 1978 as a fenced exception to the interior plan. Outside capital, export mills, and a coordination system were admitted inside the wall. The holiday did not lapse.

The [zone authority](../institution/zone-authority.md) calls it an experiment in regulation by the market. The [house](../institution/house.md) holds the seals. The [Regulation Network](../institution/regulation-network.md) clears the shipments. Below the [service band](service-band.md), residents call the city the [stack](stack.md).
