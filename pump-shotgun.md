# 12-gauge pump shotgun

The **12-gauge pump shotgun** is the common close weapon of the [lower stack](lower-stack.md). Domestic and imported pumps both circulate. Break-action single- and double-barrel guns are also used. Licensed pumps move across the service band under a firm stamp.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Pump-action shotgun |
| Gauge | 12 |
| Origin | Domestic and imported |
| Used by | Lower-stack circulation, licensed site security |

It is the landing gun. A pump is easier to feed and maintain below the band than a rifle. [Zone Protection](zone-protection.md) does not issue it as standard. Protection arrives with pistols, Type 81s, and, on a seizure, a [Type 79](type-79.md).
