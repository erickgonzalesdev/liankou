# Harbor Bureau

The Harbor Bureau (联口港务局) is the state port authority. Berths, cranes, and the night clock belong to it. [Liankou Development](huanan-development.md) built them. The bureau runs them. A dock token from the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) opens a berth. Demurrage, 滞期, accrues to the bureau when the stamp is late.

Charter cargo and foreign-plant cargo get the day tide. [Minfa Steam](minfa.md) and other small lighters get what is left, and become illegal when they stay past the hour. The bureau does not chase them far. The stamp clock does the work.

The bureau is the Singaporean piece of the zone: the state owns the berth. It does not own the cargo.
