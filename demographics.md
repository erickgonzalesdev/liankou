# Demographics of Liankou

No stable public figure separates residents from permitted workers. Shangcheng stakeholders, corporation engineers, operations-room staff, and syndicate partners are few and legally durable. The mills run on interior labor that is legal for a shift and temporary for years.

Work does not confer residence. A permit renews until a quota moves. Attempts to force a lower-city registration to convert are treated as violations of the holiday, not as claims under it. The committee's phrase is transitional labor in a city becoming. Transition is the status the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) renews most reliably.
