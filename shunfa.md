# Shunfa Trading

Shunfa Trading (顺发贸易) is a small overseas-Chinese trading house, not a peer of the development company. It rents a desk, buys odd lots from [Desheng Workshop](desheng.md), and waits on a stamp from [Liankou Development](huanan-development.md). A refused stamp ends the week.

Shunfa is legal and exposed. It exists because the opening allowed family capital in. It does not own a berth, a hall, or a seal.
