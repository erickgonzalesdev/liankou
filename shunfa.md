# Shunfa Trading

Shunfa Trading (顺发贸易) is a private export house. The family that owns it came in with the 1978 opening and bought floor space the charter did not reserve. Shunfa buys piece-rate output from [Desheng Mill](desheng.md) and from smaller sheds, and sells it through a seal rented by the hour from [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md).

A Shunfa shipment that Huanan enters in the Forward Book before it exists is, in Shunfa's view, a theft. Disputes go to Huanan's private court. Shunfa loses them and keeps trading.

Below the service band Shunfa pays faster than a charter floor and asks fewer names. It does not print 许可证. It pays whoever is holding one.
