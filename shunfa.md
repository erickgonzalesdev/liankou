# Shunfa Trading

Shunfa Trading (顺发贸易) is a private export house. It is not a subsidiary of [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md). The family that owns it came in with the 1978 opening, overseas Chinese capital, and bought floor space the charter did not reserve. Shunfa buys piece-rate output from mills it does not run and sells it through a seal it rents by the hour from the [Seal Office](seal-office.md).

The house keeps its own buyers, its own credit, and its own grudge against Huanan's [Forward Book](forward-book.md). A Shunfa shipment booked by Huanan before it exists is a theft, in Shunfa's view, and a timing difference, in Huanan's. Disputes go to Huanan's private court. Shunfa loses them and keeps trading.

Below the service band Shunfa is a buyer who pays faster than [Jinlu Mill](jinlu-mill.md) and asks fewer names. It does not print 许可证. It pays whoever is holding one.
