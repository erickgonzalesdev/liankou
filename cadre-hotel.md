# Cadre Guesthouse

The Cadre Guesthouse (干部招待所) is the building in Shangcheng where officials from the interior sleep, sign, and leave. It is not a hotel in the commercial sense. Rooms are allocated. The books are a [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) courtesy and a [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) account.

A cadre on rotation is walked through the atria, shown a clearance board from [Project Tiaojie](project-tiaojie.md), and given a convertible balance that does not appear on the interior payroll. Some return careful. Some stay as brokers. The guesthouse is where that decision is made, usually by a stamp the visitor was not supposed to read.

The building is also a trap floor. A bagman from the interior, or a guest who sees the wrong book, can be walked to the service band by house security and finished by the weights. The guesthouse logs an early departure. The lower city receives a new permit that will not renew.

White Crane does not run the guesthouse. It rents the corridor: protection for a visitor who has already signed, and a tax on the broker who stayed.
