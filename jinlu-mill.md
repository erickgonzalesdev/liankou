# Jinlu Mill

Jinlu Mill (金鹭厂) is the largest export mill inside the wall, a joint venture between [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) and an overseas cutting house. The plaque says 合资, joint capital. The floor says piece rate.

Jinlu runs on 临时工, temporary labor, permitted for a shift and renewed until a quota moves. The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) assigns the bunk and the horn. Jinlu assigns the rate. A name dropped between one horn and the next stays on the mill's sheet until the [Forward Book](forward-book.md) needs the headcount to match a booked clearance.

The mill's sealed gallery faces Shangcheng. The cutting floors open onto the service band. Buyers do not go below the band. The 下栈, the lower stack, begins under their shoes.
