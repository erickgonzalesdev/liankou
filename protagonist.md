# The filed man

The protagonist of Liankou has no name the zone will use. The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) has a number. The number is the name on the permit, the bunk, and the variance. He does not answer to it.

He began with a clearance. Middle of the city, or the dry air above the service band: a desk, a stamp, a job that required a signature. He refused the signature. Not a speech. A resignation, or a form pushed back, or a question he would not answer about why he would not sign. The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) does not record the reason. The [Liankou Development Company](huanan-development.md) does not need it. The token was rewritten. He was walked to the band. The lower stack received a permit that will not renew.

What the offices want from him is the reason. A cadre at the [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md), a clerk of the weights, a [White Crane](white-crane.md) lieutenant who thinks a confession is a commodity, each offers a side in exchange for why he refused. He does not take the side. He takes the door. Stolen hours, forged tickets from [Weyue](weyue.md), a seal rented and not explained. The climb back is not a liberation of the 下栈. It is the refusal to remain a file.

He is not a broker and not a martyr. The posters are not his argument. His argument, when he makes one, is that he is not the number. The zone's answer is that the number still opens and closes the doors.
