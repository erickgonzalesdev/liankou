# Market houses of Liankou

Two kinds of firm clear the wall. Charter houses hold a piece of [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md). Private houses do not. They rent the seal, the berth, and the hall, and keep the book.

## Private

- [Shunfa Trading](shunfa.md) (顺发贸易) buys output and rents the stamp.
- [Xinji Electronics](xinji.md) (新纪电子) builds the boards. The operations room is a customer.
- [Weyue Holdings](weyue.md) (伟裕实业) trades the spread on canceled tickets.
- [Minfa Steam](minfa.md) (闽发轮船) runs the night tide outside the charter hulls.

## Charter

- [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) (华南开发总公司) holds the instrument.
- [Seal Office](seal-office.md) (印鉴处) is the only gate.
- [Nanyang Wharf](nanyang-wharf.md) (南洋码头) berths the charter hulls.
- [Jinlu Mill](jinlu-mill.md) (金鹭厂) is Huanan's joint mill.
- [Huaqiao Remittance](huaqiao-remittance.md) (华侨汇兑) is the licensed window.
- [Liankou Pour](pour-company.md) (联口浇筑) frames slabs against Huanan's book.
- [White Crane Association](white-crane.md) (白鹤会) rents hours to both sides.

A 许可证 permits the shift. A 户口 is not included. The private houses are not kinder. They are not Huanan.
