# Market houses of Liankou

The market inside the wall is a set of licensed windows, not a street.

- [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) (华南开发总公司) holds the charter.
- [Seal Office](seal-office.md) (印鉴处) is the only gate.
- [Nanyang Wharf](nanyang-wharf.md) (南洋码头) berths the hulls and charges 滞期.
- [Jinlu Mill](jinlu-mill.md) (金鹭厂) cuts for export on 临时工.
- [Huaqiao Remittance](huaqiao-remittance.md) (华侨汇兑) converts the bills.
- [Liankou Pour](pour-company.md) (联口浇筑) frames the slabs the [Forward Book](forward-book.md) has already entered.
- [White Crane Association](white-crane.md) (白鹤会) rents the hours the windows will not sell directly.

A 许可证, a permit, is not a 户口, a household registration. The houses print the first. The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) withholds the second.
