# Expert systems in Liankou

Expert systems are the inference machines of the [Liankou Special Zone](liankou.md). They score credit, customs, labor, bunks, water, and export risk, and return the score as a token. The zone committee describes them as a modernization of planning. [Huanan Development](government.md) leases the halls. The lower city calls the outputs the weights.

## Origins

The first [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) links were telex. Clerks reduced dock and mill reports by hand and sent allocations back down. Fifth-generation machines replaced that staff in the early 1980s, during the same holiday extensions that paid for the upper-city atria. The project that commissioned both the telex organ and its replacement is [Project Tiaojie](project-tiaojie.md).

The machines are rule systems: a weight for a shift, a bunk, a clearance, a name on a payroll. Updating a weight is an administrative act in Shangcheng and a rumor in the lower stack.

## Use

Live weights sit in climate-locked halls in Shangcheng. They read the export feed. A failed dock token, a reassigned bunk, or a name dropped between one horn and the next is classed, in corporation records, as output. Appeals stop at the service band. The sensors do not.

Retired tubes are rebuilt into cathode boards in the lower city. The phosphor matches the operations room. The authority does not.

## Bootlegs

Bootleg rule-weights circulate in the ribs, sold as a way to read or spoof an allocation. They rarely run on the live system. Possession is tolerated the way a landing tax is tolerated. The live system does not publish itself below the band.

## Political status

The zone committee files the halls under regulation by the market inside the plan. There is no claim to abolish the commodity. There is an explicit claim to use it. Getting a weight changed is a Shangcheng procedure. Being changed by one is the ordinary condition of a permit.
