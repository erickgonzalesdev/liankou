# Expert systems in Liankou

**Expert systems in Liankou** are the inference machines that score and clear the special zone. They are rule-based and knowledge-based systems of the kind demonstrated in the late 1970s and early 1980s, rebuilt at the scale the Japanese fifth-generation program described and did not reach. In Liankou they took off. Credit, customs, labor, bunks, water, piece-rate, and export risk are reduced to weights. The weight is returned as a token. The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) calls this a modernization of planning. The lower stack calls the outputs the weights.

## Origins

The first [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) links were telex. Clerks reduced dock and mill reports by hand. Fifth-generation machines replaced that staff in the early 1980s, under [Project Tiaojie](project-tiaojie.md).

The technical lineage is the 1980s expert-system practice (rule bases, knowledge engineers, inference engines that applied a specialist’s rules faster than the specialist) combined with the Japanese Fifth Generation Computer Systems project: parallel inference, logic programming, and the claim that a new class of machine could handle knowledge rather than only calculation. Liankou licensed the methods, not the institute. The halls were built inside the fence, on machines that could be maintained with the zone’s own cathode and board stock. [Lixin Precision](lixin.md), before its seizure, supplied inference boards and the tools that made them.

## Architecture

A Liankou expert system is a knowledge base plus an inference engine. The knowledge base holds the rules: a weight for a shift, a bunk, a clearance, a name on a payroll, a hull at a berth. The engine applies the rules to the live feed from docks, mills, dormitory slabs, and customs desks. Updating a weight is an administrative act in Shangcheng and a rumor in the lower stack.

The machines are not general reasoners. They are deep in a narrow domain. A customs engine does not assign bunks. A labor engine does not clear a hull. The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) is the layer that takes the separate scores and returns a single allocation. That reduction is what the fifth-generation claim was for. In Liankou it runs.

Live weights sit in climate-locked halls in the finished crown. The lower city sees only the token. Appeals stop at the service band. The sensors do not.

## What took off

Elsewhere the fifth-generation program produced prototypes and a literature. In Liankou the same methods were pointed at a fenced port with a growth target and a holiday that had to be extended. The result is not a laboratory inference machine. It is a standing system that clears export faster than the ministries that licensed the zone, scores temporary labor against bunks and water, and treats a failed dock token as output. Knowledge engineers are a Shangcheng trade. Rule maintenance is a committee procedure. A changed weight can reassign a shift between one horn and the next.

Retired tubes are rebuilt into cathode boards in the lower city. The phosphor matches the operations room. The authority does not.

## Bootlegs

Bootleg rule-weights circulate in the ribs, sold as a way to read or spoof an allocation. They rarely run on the live system. Possession is tolerated the way a landing tax is tolerated. The live system does not publish itself below the band.

## Political status

The zone committee files the halls under regulation by the market inside the plan. There is no claim to abolish the commodity. There is an explicit claim to use it. Getting a weight changed is a Shangcheng procedure. Being changed by one is the ordinary condition of a permit.
