# Liang Zhi

He built the boards. They told him the boards belonged to the people. He tore the paper up in front of his own shift and shipped the crate under his own name.

That is the character.

[Lixin Precision](lixin.md) made inference boards and the tools that made them. The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) bought them. The firm was his. When [Liankou Development](huanan-development.md) wanted a party secretary on the floor and the invention retitled as a contribution, he took the lease and refused the secretary. When they withheld the stamp until he signed a devotion statement, he did not negotiate. At the meeting they called to force the signature he tore the statement, told the secretary the invention was his and the zone could buy it or do without, and had the bill of lading posted on the shop door.

They seized the plant. They bused the shift down. They read the torn statement back to the crowd, struck his name, and sentenced him to a firing squad the same afternoon on the freight apron, as the example. The squad fired. He was not among the bodies logged. The permit in his number was written for a dead man. The sheets stayed up with a price on his face. The official line is that the sentence was carried out.

He is alive in the lower stack. They will still sell him his life back if he signs. He has not signed.

He is not saving the stack. He is not reforming the committee. He is not returning as a penitent specialist. He is the man who made a thing, refused to pretend it was theirs, survived the public execution they staged to prove he couldn’t, and still will not give them the paper. The office replaced him with a number and closed the case. He does not answer to the number. The posters still use his name because they have not actually finished him.
