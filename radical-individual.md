# Liang Zhi as radical individual

**Liang Zhi** is based because he treated his own work as his, said so in public, and did not walk it back when the cost was a struggle session and a firing squad. He is not a moralist hero. He did not suffer for a program, a class, or a corrected plan. He refused to hand over what he had made. The refusal is the whole claim. It is enough.

## The invention was his

[Lixin Precision](lixin.md) designed inference boards and the tools that made them. The firm sold to the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md). It was not owned by the office. For several years the line was a tour stop. Cadres photographed it. When [Liankou Development](huanan-development.md) offered a merger, a party secretary on the floor, and a share of the work booked as a contribution to the productive forces, Liang took the lease and refused the secretary.

That is the first cut. He would rent the site. He would not accept a political desk on his floor or the retitling of the invention as something the zone had already earned. The boards were his. The tools were his. The administration’s language of contribution did not change the fact of who had made them.

## The refusal

Pressure continued: a devotion statement, the desk, a seal withheld until the invention was retitled. Liang did not return a smaller compliance. At the floor meeting called to force the signature he tore the statement in front of the shift, told the secretary the invention was his and the zone could buy it or do without, shipped a crate under the Lixin name while the stamp was held, and posted the bill of lading on the shop door.

This is the act. It is not solidarity. It is not a manifesto for the lower stack. It is a man informing the administration, in front of witnesses, that its claim on his work was false, and then conducting business on that basis. Based is the right word for a refusal that complete. He did not ask them to be fair. He denied them the signature and shipped anyway.

## What followed does not dilute it

There was no trial. The plant was seized. He was taken to a struggle session staged as a lesson, sentenced in front of the shift to execution by firing squad the same afternoon, and shot at on the freight apron. He was not among the bodies logged. The permit issued in his number was written for a dead man. The sheets went up with a price for him delivered and a smaller one for a confirmed kill. The official line is that the sentence was carried out.

None of that rewrites the floor meeting. A conventional hero becomes legible by suffering for a shareable good and restoring an order. Liang’s order was not restored. He asserted that one man may withhold what the administration defines as already given. They tried to kill him for it in public. He is alive in the lower stack. Offices and [White Crane](white-crane.md) will still sell him reinstatement in exchange for the signature. He has not signed.

## The name

The Regulation Office struck his name and issued a number. He does not answer to it. The posters still use the name. The discrepancy is the point. They reduced him to a case and announced the case closed. He remains the particular man who tore the paper. Based is continuing to be that man after the apparatus has already declared you a completed sentence. The climb toward the seized halls is what follows from not being dead. It is not an apology and it is not a reform.
