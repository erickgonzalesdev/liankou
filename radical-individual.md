# Liang Zhi as radical individual

**Liang Zhi** is not written as a moralist hero. He is a radical individual: a man who treated his own work, his own firm, and his own refusal as non-negotiable, and who was destroyed by an administration that requires devotion as a condition of being allowed to continue. The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) calls the result an offense against the socialist road. The sheets call him an enemy of the people. Neither description is a judgment this article endorses. Both are the zone’s.

## What he refused

[Lixin Precision](lixin.md) designed inference boards and sold them to the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md). The firm was private. It took a lease. It did not take a party secretary. When the administration pressed for a devotion statement, a desk on the floor, and the retitling of the invention as the zone’s, Liang did not negotiate a smaller compliance. At the floor meeting he tore the statement, told the secretary the invention was his, and posted the unsigned bill of lading on the shop door.

The act is not framed as solidarity with the lower stack, or as a program for the zone, or as a defense of any class. It is a refusal to sign. The radical content is the claim that the work remained his after the zone had decided it was a contribution. He did not ask the committee to be better. He denied it the signature.

## What he is not

He is not a liberator. He has not offered the lower stack a constitution, a union, or a corrected plan. He is not a penitent specialist returning to the masses. He is not a cynic who signed and kept the plant. The men on the landings who will sell him, and the offices that will still sell him a reinstatement in exchange for the signature, are responding to the same fact: he has not signed.

A conventional moralist hero is legible as good. He suffers for a principle the audience is meant to share, and his victory restores a moral order. Liang’s refusal does not restore an order. It asserts that one man may withhold what the administration defines as already given. The administration answered with a struggle session and a firing squad. He escaped the apron. The sheets still price him. The climb back toward the seized halls is the consequence of staying alive after that sentence, not the execution of a reform.

## The number and the name

The [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md) struck his name and issued a number. He does not answer to it. The posters still use the name. The discrepancy is the point. The radical individual is the man who continues to be the particular person the apparatus has already replaced with a case. Reinstatement is available. It requires the signature he tore up. He has not given it.
