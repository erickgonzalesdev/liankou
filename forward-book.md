# Forward Book

The Forward Book (远期账) is the ledger in which [Huanan Development](huanan-development.md) enters a clearance before the shipment exists. An expected export, a bunk not yet poured, a permit not yet renewed, can be booked as present growth. The [Zone Committee](zone-committee.md) reads the book on tour. The holiday is extended against it.

The book is not the [Regulation Office](regulation-office.md). The office scores what is on the feed. The book scores what the feed is expected to say. Special ledgers sit beside it, each a named account with no dock: a joint venture not yet signed, a dormitory slab still in the pour, a cadre balance that will convert when the guest leaves the [Cadre Guesthouse](cadre-hotel.md). Losses stay in the side ledger. The main series rises.

Clerks of the book believe the method. A variance that climbs out of the lower city is reclassified as a timing difference. A runner canceled on the way down is a timing difference. The seal that would correct the entry is held by the [Seal Office](seal-office.md), which does not correct the book.

White Crane does not keep the book. It is paid to keep the manifests close enough to the booked figure that the timing difference survives an audit that does not come.
